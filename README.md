# MARKET FORCES
A 2D learning RPG that teaches the major economic systems of the last two thousand years through play. The second game from Bella Vita Labs.
Ten chapters. Five systems that ran the world: Capitalism, Socialism, the Command Economy, the Mixed Economy, and Mercantilism. Five ideas that never fully arrived: Georgism, Technocracy, Cybernetic Planning, the Gift Economy, and the Resource Based Economy.
Each chapter you walk a living town shaped by its system, gather insights, study a balanced bookshelf with real books arguing both sides, then run the economy yourself at the Decision Desk, where four needles (Prosperity, Equality, Freedom, Stability) show every tradeoff honestly. Clear the chapter and a Free Play sandbox unlocks: pull the levers and see if your world thrives forever or collapses in a moment.
The game takes no side. Comprehension wins, ideology does not.
